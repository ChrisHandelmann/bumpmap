Drives are stored only on this phone, in this browser. Export now and then to keep a backup or to merge data from another phone.

Tips

  • Mount the phone firmly (dash or vent mount). A loose phone in a cupholder rattles and inflates readings. Keep the same mount for comparable maps.
  • Tap Start before you pull out, then leave the phone alone. The screen stays on while recording; locking it or switching apps pauses recording.
  • Segments below 15 km/h, with weak GPS, or where the phone moved in its mount are skipped.
  • Bumpiness = vertical vibration (RMS, m/s²) over each 20 m, scaled to what it would be at 40 km/h.
